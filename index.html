CardSphere AI is designed for collectors, shops, and serious hobbyists who want card decisions to feel organized, explainable, and trustworthy.

01

Evidence-first scanning

Capture a card, review OCR evidence, compare candidates, and decide what to save. The workflow is built around review before action.

02

Portfolio intelligence

Understand collection health, confidence gaps, risk signals, and next steps so your collection feels easier to reason about.

03

Local-first collection management

Your collection starts on your device. Core workflows are designed to work without an account, keeping local control at the center.

04

Optional cloud sync

Cloud sync is optional and user-controlled. It is designed for account-backed continuity when a collector chooses to enable it.

05

Provider-aware market signals

Market context is designed around authorized, partner, licensed, user-entered, cached, or demo data sources. No marketplace scraping.

06

Built for trust

Trust Center guidance, local-first controls, careful provider wording, and clear limits help keep decisions grounded.
