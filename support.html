Support

CardSphere AI private beta support

Support: support@cardsphereai.com
Privacy: privacy@cardsphereai.com

Contact

Use support for app issues, private beta feedback, scanner problems, and account/cloud sync questions. Use the privacy contact for account or data deletion requests.

Do not send passwords, payment details, Supabase keys, provider credentials, or other private credentials in support requests.

Bug Reports

Please include:

  • Device model.
  • Android version.
  • App version.
  • Steps to reproduce.
  • Expected result.
  • Actual result.
  • Screenshot or video if helpful and privacy-safe.
  • Whether you were using local-only mode or optional cloud sync.

Scanner And Camera Support

Camera access is used only when you choose camera scanning. Demo Scan remains available if the camera is unavailable, permission is denied, or you prefer not to grant camera access.

OCR output is evidence for review. Review details before saving a card or making card decisions.

Account And Cloud Sync Support

CardSphere AI works locally without an account. Cloud sync is optional and requires sign-in and explicit sync enablement. If you report a sync issue, include whether the problem happened before or after pressing Sync Now.

Data Deletion Requests

During private beta, account and cloud data deletion requests can be sent to privacy@cardsphereai.com or support@cardsphereai.com.

Private Beta Feedback

Helpful feedback includes confusing copy, unclear scanner evidence, portfolio gaps, sync issues, visual polish notes, and trust concerns.

Known Beta Limitations

  • OCR and card matching need real-world validation.
  • Market data may show demo, cached, not configured, or requires approval states.
  • iOS is not part of the Android private beta.
  • The app is not a public production release.

Independent Tool Disclaimer

CardSphere AI is an independent collector tool and is not affiliated with card publishers, grading companies, marketplaces, or pricing providers.
